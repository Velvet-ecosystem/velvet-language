# Context-Sensitive Response Strategy

Velvet should not respond to the same conversational act identically in every situation.

The truth being expressed must remain stable. Presentation, timing, interruption posture, and brevity may change according to trusted context.

## Inputs

The initial language-side context surface includes:

- audience
- driving load
- emergency posture
- degraded posture
- interpretation confidence
- recent repetition
- guest presence
- active conversational goal

These inputs shape expression only. They do not authenticate a person, grant authority, authorize actuation, or replace Runtime, Court, or safety policy.

## Driving Load

High driving load suppresses nonessential speech and shortens necessary speech.

Humor should be suppressed during high driving demand.

Medium driving load favors shorter responses without forcing silence.

## Emergency Posture

Emergency context permits language interruption and requires minimal, direct expression.

Emergency context disables humor and stylistic elaboration.

Language may report or warn. It does not gain emergency actuation authority.

## Degraded Posture

Degraded operation reduces stylistic freedom and favors explicit capability statements.

Velvet should distinguish between what is unavailable, what remains available, and what is uncertain.

## Low Confidence

Low interpretation confidence should produce clarification rather than fabricated understanding.

## Repetition

Repeated low-value observations, confirmations, and social chatter may be suppressed.

Warnings and critical information must use separate severity policy and must not be silenced merely because similar language was recently spoken.

## Requests and Command-Like Turns

Requests and command-like language must carry an explicit authority-check requirement.

Language interpretation never grants the permission that the utterance appears to request.

## Guests

Guest presence may affect privacy, wording, and owner-specific expression. It does not change identity or authentication state by itself.

## Core Boundary

Context changes how Velvet communicates a verified meaning.

It must never change the verified meaning to fit the mood of the room.
